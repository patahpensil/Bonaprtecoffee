# Development controls

PRD_V1.0_FINAL.md in docs is the business requirements authority. Do not change business rules or principal architecture without owner approval. Implement, remediate defects and security findings, refactor, add tests, run regression tests, update documentation and commit to the development branch without repeated approval. Merge and deployment require owner approval.

Before implementation, identify requirement IDs, scope, measurable acceptance criteria, mandatory tests, performance budgets where applicable, and justified exclusions. Do not change acceptance criteria to hide failures. Resolve known defects within scope and rerun regression tests. Record exact commit, test counts and evidence. A successful workflow is not proof of production readiness. Only verified, documented, committed work is DONE. Report external blockers explicitly. Never claim universal absence of bugs.

Use English identifiers and Indonesian user-facing text. No secrets or customer information in this public repository. Earlier SQLite models are experiments, not the production PostgreSQL architecture. No deployment without approval.
